2 · First-time authorization, only if necessary

Keep Mudra connected to Windows/macOS as a HID mouse. Chrome may require a one-time chooser even when the OS already paired it. The chooser can discover nearby BLE devices; select only your existing Mudra. This page never initiates OS pairing or a general unfiltered scan.

If Mudra isn't listed, that's a meaningful result: Chrome's Web Bluetooth chooser may not see a device already connected to the OS HID stack. Try the other filters, but do not unpair it. Never grant access to another device to make the test pass.
